# Can you turn an Insta360 camera without losing the detail you meant to capture?

**Testing plan, not results.** Keep the two native lens views. Start with one X6 and one recording mode. The decision is whether a reusable mask per physical lens fixes the orientation-dependent loss, or whether the remaining error calls for better projection, rig calibration, or fringe correction. No product change, camera-wide default, or new ingest pipeline follows from a single clip.

## What the experiment must distinguish

| Possible cause | Prediction on previously unseen footage | What would argue against it |
|---|---|---|
| The automatic fit follows the scene rather than the optical edge. | The fitted border moves relative to the **visible raw border** between captures; transferring an unchanged fit recovers good detail or excludes more bad pixels. | The raw footprint itself moves, or the transferred fit is no better. A stable but consistently wrong fit is a different failure. |
| One circle and shrink value cannot describe this lens's usable rim. | A hand-tuned, fixed per-lens shape beats both automatic and transferred circles, especially in repeatable sectors. | The same fixed shape cuts clean detail or leaks bad pixels under other lighting or orientations. |
| Good pixels are being assigned the wrong rays or lens poses. | Both masks retain the target, but clean features at the rim misproject. Error that persists on distant targets suggests projection or rotation; extra depth-dependent error suggests testing the baseline. | A mask-only change fixes the held-out reconstruction. Near/far behavior is a discriminator, not proof of a particular rig offset. |
| The sensor/ISP output has unusable colour at the rim. | In single-lens originals, edge-channel displacement repeats with position; or a halo appears mainly at clipped highlights. | Displacement appears only after extraction, stitching, or rendering, or doesn't repeat. Investigate that stage instead. |

**Known starting point.** The [fitter](../../src/app/FrameMask.cpp#L929-L1018) uses temporal activity, then darkness, **once per camera group**, not once per video frame. Its [defaults](../../src/app/FrameMask.h#L73-L96) are up to 24 frames, dark luma 16, 1% radial shrink; record actual settings. [Saved stencil sets](frame-stencil.md#per-camera) are drawn shapes, not a history of automatic fits. The [trailer reader](../../src/sfm/core/Telemetry.cpp#L1333-L1336) reports `offset_v3` presence, not a validated Insta360 camera model. The [DJI conversion](../../src/sfm/core/LensCalibration.h#L26-L44) assumes a specific projection polynomial. The [dual-fisheye rig](sfm-rig-constraints.md#known-lens-geometry-and-matching) starts back-to-back; rotation is refined and translation normally constrained to the optical axis. An image centre in pixels, a rotation, and a baseline in millimetres are three different measurements.

## 1. Select captures and freeze the comparison

| Order | Inputs | What they can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot: X6, one unit/mode | Two independent camera-original captures, **CAL** and **TEST**, with both physical lenses. Use an existing same-unit pair if suitable. | Can a profile made on CAL transfer to TEST? No claim about other units. |
| Confirmation: X4a, then X4 | Repeat CAL/TEST on one unit/mode each. Add one **new X6 TEST** with different rim content; don't retune the X6 profile. | Does the fix waste good X4a detail, fail a different lens offset, or only work on the first X6 scene? Report each unit separately. |
| Before a model default | Hold out a second physical unit of that model, and separately qualify each proposed mode/crop/guard configuration. | Model-level transfer. Without this evidence recommend only a unit/mode profile or an opt-in. |

For a short new capture, plan about 20–30 seconds per take. In CAL, include textured neutral edges and a bright unsaturated background at the rim, looking through both lenses. In TEST put a distant sign or fine texture and a nearer thin textured object where they cross the overlap. Slowly turn through the crossing in both directions; include a tilted pass to put it in another sector. Put blank sky/wall, dark **real** texture, and a bright high-contrast edge at parts of the rim. **Also walk a short lateral arc and revisit the targets**: a rotation in place is useful for masks but a poor SfM test. Keep the scene static; mark occlusion, motion blur, and clipped highlights instead of treating them as calibration errors. If existing clips lack one condition, report which question they cannot answer; a split inside one clip is only a rehearsal for transfer.

Use a fresh private scratch directory per campaign. Originals and existing datasets are read-only. Record a private manifest: anonymized unit/physical-lens IDs, original hash, clip role, firmware, mode, resolution, fps, lens guards, stabilization/crop, exposure and colour profile, executable revision/hash, decoder and extraction settings. Check physical lens ↔ track/folder and stored orientation from an asymmetric landmark; don't assume `cam0` always means front. Verify stable raw footprint across frames independently of the fitted circle. Split incompatible modes instead of scaling profiles by filename or pooling serials. Keep recordings, raw serials, GPS, paths, calibration strings and identifying images out of Git.

**Decode once per clip.** Prefer existing identical extracted pixels when available. Otherwise use Spirula's synchronized dual-track route without pano conversion, adaptive selection or model masking. Example for a short 30 fps TEST, using local aliases, in a fresh scratch root:

```sh
spirula sam extract TEST.insv --360 off --sync --skip 10 --end 20 --quality -1 --out test/images --report test-extract.csv
```

Use the same recipe on CAL and other arms; `--quality -1` saves PNG, not a lossless camera original. Record any LUT explicitly (especially X6 I-Log), range, rotation and scale. Match the actual GUI decode/colour settings for the user-path check. For different carrier layouts, first verify extraction yields **two** correct synchronized lens views; don't treat a proxy or stitched panorama as a raw lens. Require matching capture times/stems, sizes and pixel hashes across arms, at least 24 sampled instants per lens for the default fit, and enough frames through the crossing. Before large runs estimate scratch for full-resolution image pairs, sidecars and checkpoints; choose a shorter representative take if necessary, not a lower-resolution rim test.

## 2. Three cheap mask comparisons; no training yet

Use the existing [mask command](../../src/app/cli/sam_main.cpp#L683-L748) on the same TEST image tree. No SAM weights, object masks, alpha, previous sidecars or `feature_masks/`. Every output directory starts empty, outside `images/`. Run CAL separately, never fit a profile on TEST.

| Arm | How to produce it | What the comparison isolates |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto** | Fit CAL and TEST independently with the same settings. Save `--print` output per camera and the written PNGs. | What today's per-capture fit actually did; compare in pixels on the source frames. |
| **Transferred auto** | Take one verified, static **CAL Auto raster per lens** and reuse it byte-for-byte as the correctly named sidecar for every TEST frame of that lens. Verify dimensions, orientation and pixel hash. | Difference from Auto isolates refitting, without manual improvements or a different rasterizer. If CAL did not find a border, this arm is unavailable. |
| **Fixed profile** | Using **only CAL**, open Draw/Try the mask, separate the cameras, **Edit border ellipse** for each lens, adjust centre/axes, remove a sector only where CAL justifies it, then save the named per-camera SVG set. Apply it unchanged to TEST. | Difference from transferred auto tests whether changing the shape/usable boundary helps, not just whether it is stable. |

```sh
spirula sam mask test/images --samples 24 --dark 16 --shrink 0.01 --print
spirula sam mask test/images --samples 24 --dark 16 --shrink 0.01 --out test/auto
spirula sam mask test/images --shape profiles/rim-cam0.svg --out test/fixed
```

Use the saved SVG's actual name; keep same-title sibling files together. Association is by embedded `data-camera` matching the relative camera folder, not by the chosen filename. `--shape` supplies the whole stencil; `--print` alone writes no PNG. Score `ARM/camN/<frame-stem>.png` at stored image size, white = keep. Check every expected name, size, polarity and lens key; spot-check overlays on original pixels. A logged **Auto no-fit** and no other mask means effectively keep-all: count it as such, not as a successful detection. An unexplained missing mask or unmatched Fixed lens invalidates that arm. The GUI video preview samples differently; it isn't the batch-fit measurement. Finally reproduce the winning setup in actual GUI dataset creation (fixed areas on, object segmentation off, fitting off for each frozen lens) and compare generated `masks/` pixels; don't assume a preset or preview applied it.

## 3. Score what matters, not percent masked

Before viewing arm overlays, mark CAL and TEST raw-frame crops at native resolution. Preselect at least 12 synchronized instants per TEST across both crossings/tilts and difficult lighting, with centre, mid-field and rim samples on **both** lenses. Label clearly usable scene (including dark texture), definitely black/barrel, visibly unusable fringe, and ambiguous/occluded. Use the same labelled patches/pixels for every arm. Include azimuth sectors around the rim rather than letting large easy black corners or bright central pixels dominate. Keep a few cropped contact sheets: raw, label, then randomized arm overlays. Repeat a blind subset of CAL labels; before opening TEST scores write the per-rate tolerance from that label variation and the target crossings that must be preserved. Ambiguity stays unscored; record its size.

| Readout | Calculation / interpretation |
|---|---|
| Good detail thrown away | `usable pixels excluded / labelled usable pixels`, for rim and centre separately. Lower is better. |
| Bad pixels admitted | `black/barrel pixels kept / labelled black/barrel pixels`; separately `unusable fringe kept / labelled unusable fringe`. Never pool these into one score. |
| Coverage through the turn | Follow the **same identifiable target** at each preselected instant. Where either raw lens has labelled usable detail, count instants neither mask keeps it; also report where both keep it. Split near/far and distinguish a real occlusion or an intrinsically blind direction. Do not score near objects by assuming the lenses share a centre. |
| Repeatability | For Auto, record no-fits and `(cx,cy,rx,ry)` in the stored pixel grid and normalized units. Overlay on raw edges. Movement by 0.5% alone proves neither bad masking nor a wrong projection. |

Report numerators/denominators **per clip, lens and condition**, not millions of pixels as independent trials. A missing class is “not tested,” not zero error. Sanity-check the scorer with keep-all and reject-all on the same labels (opposite 0/100% errors). To advance a mask, it must visibly rescue a named target crossing or improve a predeclared rim-error rate beyond CAL relabelling variation, with no new avoidable coverage gap, and no increase beyond tolerance in the other error rates or in centre loss on either lens. If two arms tie, prefer the existing behavior. An improvement on one lens must not conceal damage to the other. Mark trades and marginal results **inconclusive**; do not retune on TEST and call it held out.

## 4. Does the detail survive reconstruction?

Only run this comparison for Auto versus the **simplest successful mask**, or on one well-labelled clean-pixel case to diagnose residual misalignment. Use the native non-NVIDIA Vulkan route. Freeze the user's dataset recipe, binary/device, colour settings, image resolution, lens grouping and rig/telemetry inputs. Keep geometry/depth priors, exposure handling and feature settings identical. Select the eval time blocks **before** SfM: both lenses from one instant go to the same split. Use scratch filename tags containing `train` / `eval`, preserve lens pairing, temporal order and any manifest references, and record the name list. Eval images are held out of splat training, not independent SfM poses.

1. **SfM:** run fresh `spirula sfm auto` for each arm with the same images/manifest and its own `--masks` and empty workspace. Preserve the production fisheye model, per-lens intrinsics and dual-fisheye rig. Do not reuse `features/`, `matches.bin` or just run `sfm map`: masks change feature extraction. Check mask lookup/drop logs; exclude or identically pin feature-only masks and alpha. Record expected/registered image **names** per lens and component, largest usable connected trajectory, and rim versus centre track support and reprojection residuals on comparable observations. More points or lower in-sample RMS after dropping hard views isn't a win.
2. **Trainer:** use `spirula train 3dgs` on the selected COLMAP component with the matching sidecars. Pin `--load-masks 1 --apply-loss-for-mask 0 --eval-mode filename --save-eval-images 1 --keep-viewer-alive 0`, nonzero sampling seed, training budget, splat cap, resolution, mask-boundary offset and both warp settings. For native-pixel crops use `--warp-to-pinhole 0 --warp-spherical-to-pinhole 0` consistently. Keep one centre/interior control. Record resolved configs and the init cloud: each arm's native cloud for end-to-end behavior. If attribution matters, run the mask-only trainer comparison on **one fixed reconstruction, camera set and seed cloud**, changing only training masks. This separates training supervision from a changed SfM solution.
3. **Evaluate independently of the training mask:** in the trainer's sidecar tree use arm-specific masks for training names, but the **same** independently labelled evaluation masks for the held-out names in both arms. Leave the SfM sidecar trees unchanged. Compare saved ground truth (GT) and renders on the same usable rim/target patches and centre controls, including detail excluded by a candidate. Use per-view uncorrected RGB L1 and paired enlarged crops; whole-image PSNR/SSIM are secondary. Verify identical GT pixels and join each numbered render to its original lens/frame, for example by uniquely matching its saved GT to the evaluation inputs at the resolved resolution and colour transform. An ambiguous join is not a paired score. [Eval output](../../src/app/TrainerCore.cpp#L2748-L2762) can skip fully masked views. Report missing/unregistered names against the **full planned list**; never turn their loss into a better mean. Distinct components without shared poses are not one trajectory.
4. **Decision:** look for the same recovered detail through the turn, no new doubled or coloured edges, no lost required views, and no centre regression. Repeat an unchanged baseline to estimate render variation; confirm close results with a second matched nonzero seed. Require paired improvement outside repeat variation, or report a mask-only improvement with downstream benefit unproven. If a crop improves only by changing pose or warping the eval image, check it on a common camera/GT grid before attributing a gain. See the [split](../datasets.md#traineval-split), [mask semantics](../datasets.md#masks), and [repeatability evidence](sfm-quality-experiments.md#rendering-repeats-are-necessary).

## 5. Conditional tests, not the initial workload

| Trigger | Test that could change the decision | Evidence required before recommending a change |
|---|---|---|
| A fixed profile transfers, but automatic selection is needed; or clean pixels still misproject. | Inspect one **unedited original** of each model/mode for metadata presence/version. The [field list and uncertainties](imu-gps-for-sfm.md) are a lead, not a spec. Establish lens-block order, units, reference resolution/crop/orientation, pixel-centre convention, projection law, and rotation order/handedness against independent multi-pose target observations spanning centre and rim. Hold out target poses from fitting. | A physically plausible, one-to-one pixel↔ray map throughout the usable region, low held-out reprojection error per radial/azimuth bin relative to the existing fit and repeat noise; round-tripping your own formula isn't validation. Compare baseline vs factory **intrinsic initializers only** at fixed masks and identical refinement. Unknown, stripped, malformed, mode-mismatched or ambiguous metadata must leave the normal fit/manual profile usable. Do not assume every model writes `offset_v3` or pass its coefficients to the DJI formula. |
| Field-angle cutting seems preferable to a pixel profile. | Only after the ray map is verified, map labelled clean/black/fringe samples to off-axis angle and azimuth. Plot what is recoverable and the two far-field view footprints in one rig frame. Pick cutoffs on CAL, then compare the angular mask with the pixel profile on TEST using the same error and coverage gates. | No new holes in actually observable directions; report solid-angle coverage by at least one lens and the overlap covered by both. Allow asymmetric sectors if justified. Neither 90° nor 95° is a universal setting; factory geometry does not measure image quality. |
| Single-lens geometry works; good cross-lens targets still double, particularly up close. | Keep masks and intrinsics fixed. First change only relative rotation initialization if the source frames/conventions were independently identified. Separately test current axial vs physically measured or unconstrained translation, using a known-size target and both near and distant held-out observations. | Repeatable improvement by depth without an interior regression. Yaw/pitch/roll aren't a translation vector; an unscaled SfM baseline is not millimetres. Without independent scale/observability, report that physical offset is unresolved, not an X4a hardware diagnosis. |
| X6 usable overlap is still too narrow because of colour. | On unsaturated achromatic edges measure signed red–green and blue–green edge displacement versus radius/azimuth; include centre and a second take/exposure. Inspect decoded original vs working frames for LUT, JPEG or chroma-upsampling artifacts. Compare with halo width on clipped highlights. | Only test an offline per-channel correction if a stable spatial displacement repeats. Fit on CAL, verify on TEST against an identically resampled uncorrected control: reduced shift, no centre blur, and **more usable target overlap**. Highlight-only purple flare or a nonrepeatable shift calls for exclusion/uncertainty, not global channel scaling. |

If available, a matched Insta360 Studio export at the same times/orientations is a **visual reference** for where detail exists. Record its stitch/stabilization settings. A seam invisible in a blended panorama is not ground truth for rays, baseline, or close-object geometry. Stitched-input training and soft loss weighting are not prerequisites; the latter would not fix SfM accepting invalid features.
